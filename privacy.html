Privacy policy

Effective October 4, 2026.

Content Factory Media Archive is a private backup utility operated by the Google account owner. It accesses Google Drive to upload, list, verify, and restore production media and backup manifests in the configured archive folders. Google account information is used to verify the connected account.

The OAuth permission allows Google Drive access; the utility’s configuration restricts its operations to the designated Content Factory archive. It does not publish backed-up files, sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or train artificial-intelligence models on it.

Production media is stored in the operator’s private Google Drive. OAuth credentials and verification records are stored in permission-restricted files on the operator’s production server. Public website visitors have no access to these files. This website does not use analytics or tracking scripts; GitHub Pages may process access logs under GitHub’s own privacy policy.

Archives remain until the operator removes them. The operator can revoke OAuth access in Google Account settings, delete archive files from Drive, and remove local credentials at any time. The utility’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

For questions or removal requests, contact the operator. Do not post tokens, credentials, or private files in public issues.
